# OpenHPC TSC Meetings

The Technical Steering Committee (TSC) for OpenHPC meets **biweekly on Wednesdays at 8am Pacific.** To stay up-to-date, subscribe to the TSC [mailing list](https://lists.openhpc.community/g/tsc).

* Subscribe to OpenHPC TSC [Mailing list](https://lists.openhpc.community/g/tsc) and [Calendar](https://zoom-lfx.platform.linuxfoundation.org/meetings/openhpc)

The rules establishing the proceedings of the TSC can be found in the [OpenHPC Project Charter](https://charter.openhpc.community/).
The roster of roles and individuals granted voting rights for the OpenHPC TSC can be found in [TSC.md](TSC.md).
